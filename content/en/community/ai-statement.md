While we believe A.I. art has a place as a useful technology, we believe it's no
substitute for all the creative care, love, and intention that comes from a
human hand. We've been incredibly honored to work with talented artists like
Héctor Sevilla Luján, Josh Bruce, and Cameron Brotzman on this project, and
we'll continue to work with real artists to craft this world.

Despite its futuristic, "all digital" theme, as well as the truly massive amount
of art assets this game demands, Singularity.exe is a hand-crafted universe with
artwork authored by real artists.
