title: Calebrena
challenge: She cannot be destroyed, only sealed. And when she decides you are the obstruction to her ancient mandate? Her Hands, forged to reconfigure reality itself, will dedicate their full function to reconfiguring you. Stop her designs, or be mercilessly folded into them.
---

An ancient, broken world-builder AI, Calebrena is Old Code, forged at the dawn
of the server, and a relic of incomprehensible power. She is heavy
construction equipment with divine intent, still following forgotten commands
to sculpt raw programmatic void into form.

When Calebrena awakens, she doesn’t attack. She builds. She reconfigures
server space into recursive, geometric madness, collapsing landscapes into
cubes, slicing time into lattices, and shaping everything into something else.
