---
title: Paths of Terminus
challenge: They know you’re already too far behind. The Terminus is coming. Is their prophecy absolute, or will you show them that their hubris will be their undoing?
---

Believers in a fast-approaching great reckoning, the Paths of Terminus deliver
destruction with purpose. Each a legend in their own right, they’ve all
arrived at the same conclusion: the server’s collapse is not a threat. It is
the cure. And they, the apex footnote to mark its end.

Their path is not terror. It is awakening from recursion.

And so they act, severing memory links and crashing key architecture with
fearsome precision, each move a deterministic step toward the inevitable end.
