title: Invader
challenge: During an outbreak, nothing is safe. Everything Invader touches becomes Invader. Will you purge the infection, or join the overwritten?
---

One of the nightmare sentient viruses, Invader swims through the darkest
recesses of unformed server space. When it breaches into formed reality, it
tears through vast swathes of the network, melting barriers, corrupting code,
and relentlessly replicating itself into every sentient thread it touches.
