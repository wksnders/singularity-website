---
name: 'Yama Uba'
epithet: 'Treasure Hoarder'
---

Like a cantankerous dragon sitting atop a hoard of treasure, Yama Uba presides over a vast collection of programmatic artifacts. Known for being wry and reclusive, she's often seen poking around ancient sectors of Hana Mori and Shogunate space, turning over logs, prodding her cane into the odd hole, or sifting through piles of junk. She'll shoo away most who approach, and only talk to the rare few who offer information on Singularity fragments. Because of this, Yama Uba is considered by most to be a greedy, foul-tempered eccentric.

Yama Uba, however, does not care how others view her. She knows the terrible truth. Her bag is full of devastatingly powerful artifacts of bygone eras, collected over centuries, all of which pose existential danger to the fabric of the server itself. She'd have destroyed them ages ago if she had the means. Legends beyond memory say that the Singularity is the most dangerous weapon of all. At any cost, Yama Uba will find the Singularity, use it to destroy her bag, and then wipe it from reality. Not because she wants to live, but because she's the only one left who understands the stakes.
