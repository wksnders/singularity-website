---
name: 'Scrapper'
epithet: 'Queen of the Broken Heap'
---

Scrapper was always known as a weird kid, growing up alone on the edge of the Broken Heap in an obscure and empty part of Monarchy territory. While most regarded the Heap as a union of graveyard and junkyard, twice as unpleasant as both, Scrapper took to it as a playground for her imagination — an endless treasure trove to be explored and cherished.

In time, no one had ventured as far into its heart, or mapped as much of its infinite contents as Scrapper, not even Shogunate Archivists or Hana Mori's Grave Watch.

Her workshop was always lighting up at night, blinking in the dark as flurries of sparks lit the windows against the silhouetted mountains of junk behind them. During the day, passersby would see the impression of a young girl wearing a welding helmet against the sky, scrabbling atop the mountains of shredded and broken code.

At 16, Scrapper transformed the world. Releasing Scrap Brigade, one of the most brilliant code collections in a generation, Scrapper reminded the server that junk still sparkles. Older and more mature now, she acts as a mentor to Monarchy's misguided youth, giving them purpose, fascination, and direction in the repurposing of the beautifully abandoned.

In her wanderings through the Heap, few have been as close to the Old Code as Scrapper, even if most of it doesn't work. Scrap Brigade integrated some elements of broken Old Code. The Singularity? Scrapper's not out to change the world. She just wants to fix what's in front of her, and teach others to do the same. She wants to hit it with a wrench and find out what makes it tick! No one program can help everyone, but a billion of them might do the trick.
