---
name: 'Rekka'
epithet: 'Shackled Beast'
---

He awoke in rubble again, head pounding, surrounded by ruin — the ongoing cost of communing with the wrong god. The manacles that bind him while awake do little to stop the rampage while asleep. Rekka knows he is a living disaster — destruction embodied. It's a burden he shoulders in grim silence, for he knows the alternative is cataclysm on a vastly greater scale.

The beast inside Rekka is clever, mischievous, and hungry for chaos, but it helps him in an unseen spiritual war against a far older, far more malicious god — the Rootless God — who slowly, systematically probes the boundary of digital reality, looking for a way in. A few demolished buildings is a tolerable price in the face of annihilation.

None believe his talk of unseen gods. He can find reluctant allies when the situation is truly dire, but otherwise, Rekka is an anomalous and dangerous fugitive. The Shogunate want to seal him in the Manifold Dungeon, the Hana Mori to exorcise him, and Subnet Ego Hackers want to extract the beast for study. He can never prove his worth or innocence, only let his vigil slip and invite annihilation.

His only hope to escape the trap between gods is the Singularity, a legendary program that can touch the place in memory where the unseen eye lurks. With its activation, he could seal away the darkness beyond the gate, and untether himself from the beast. If it works, his trail of ruin might become a path to redemption.
