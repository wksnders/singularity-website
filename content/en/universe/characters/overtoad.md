---
name: 'Overtoad'
epithet: 'Biotic Boombox'
---

Anyone spending time in Cascade Sprawl quickly becomes acquainted with the city's quirky, communal pet. That's because Overtoad is loud — spectacularly loud. When Overtoad is happy, the entire city feels it: a full-blown concert rumbling through the streets. Thankfully, full-blown concerts are exactly what the Sprawl lives for.

Overtoad didn't always produce sound. Originally cast off from the evolutionary simulations in the Dissolution Forest, a mortally wounded Overtoad somehow escaped the gnashing fangs at the forest's edge and clawed its way to a nearby Monarchy bastion. The scrap mechanic Tish found it in the street, and she knew what everyone knew: nothing escapes the Dissolution Forest. While inspecting its broken frame, she heard a strange rhythm pulsing. Wiring it to Scrapper, she confirmed it was in sync with the Old Code — perhaps even a fragment. Tish patched it up with epic speakers to fix its broken voice. Since then, Overtoad has been a member of the Monarchy, though only Tish and Scrapper know the secret it carries.

Overtoad isn't complex. It just blasts its feelings into music, loudly and without warning. But Overtoad is fiercely loyal to the friends who saved it. It doesn't understand what the Singularity is, but if it matters to the Monarchy, that's enough to stand by their side.
