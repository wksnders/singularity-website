---
name: 'Satellite 137'
epithet: 'Ancient Relic'
---

Of all relics of bygone wars, Satellite 137 is one of the most enigmatic. Found floating at the highest reaches of server space by Zodiac Archivists, no one could decipher its code after months of research. All anyone could say for sure is that deep within its program, 137 contained an embedded fragment of the Singularity. Tired of hearing cautionary advice from timid scholars, Kagemusha ordered 137 to be activated, eager to see its destructive potential.

Upon awakening, 137 demonstrated keen, if silent, intelligence, as well as an eerily selective obedience. It promptly follows most orders issued by those with the Shogunate sign, but not all.

Studying its behavior from afar, Scrapper believes Satellite 137 is still following some ancient directive beyond comprehension — one that involves assembling the Singularity. Regardless of 137's true motive or purpose, many in the Shogunate readily welcome such a formidable and imposing ally.
