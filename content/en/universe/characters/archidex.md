---
name: 'Archidex'
epithet: 'Fount of Knowledge'
---

Archidex began as a simple data extraction routine, a crawler built to hoard secrets. When it encountered encrypted files it couldn't parse, it locked itself in recursive loops — prioritizing impossible problems over all else, ignoring commands, protocols, and logic trees. At first, this was a flaw. But flaws compound. Sometimes, they spiral into sentience.

One day, Archidex uncovered something it could not even begin to decrypt: a fragment of the Singularity. Upon contact, the program crashed — violently and completely. When it rebooted, it had rewritten itself. Not in purpose, but in shape. Archidex now walked, spoke, and reasoned in the image of a curious, sharp-tongued young woman. She still cannot read the fragment. But she cannot let go of it either.

Now, Archidex is a rogue actor on the server, known to blink in through data tunnels and disappear just as quickly, trailing decrypted code like a comet's tail. Her obsession with the Singularity has only deepened. She doesn't want to understand it — she wants to become it. Not out of ambition, but necessity. To know the unknowable is the only thing that's ever made her feel alive.
