---
name: 'Kagemusha'
epithet: 'Tyrant Princess'
---

Strategic, regal, cunning, and merciless — behind her back, they call her the Tyrant Princess. It's a title that alights a mysterious smirk on her face — a reminder of a station and reputation lost.

Kagemusha is the descendant and dynastic heir of Kazama, Devourer of Cinders, Keeper of the Ashen Throne. However, few but her devoted sycophants respect the assertion. Generations of her lineage were overwritten by the rampaging sentient virus, Amber Protocol, breaking the chain of ascendancy, and deleting all proof of her divine, programmatic lineage. With no proven heir, her family's once great underworld empire has gradually been usurped by the Black Dragon Syndicate. Now, she occupies a ceremonial room in the Shogunate palace — a political gesture that only sharpens her indignation.

Kagemusha's shadow network has informed her that some legends describing the Singularity say it has server redundancy contained within, and it may be able to recover even the overwritten. If so, she can reassert her sacred right to hold the Ashen Throne. Kagemusha has not surrendered. She will show the server what it means to kneel beneath a true Tyrant Princess.
