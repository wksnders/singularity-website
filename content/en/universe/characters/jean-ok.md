name: 'Jean O.K.'
epithet: 'Reckless Bioneer'

Jean Odessa Koncavia has intensely vivid dreams. In the most frequent, she floats deep beneath the ocean. Sunlight filters down through the surface, her long orange hair drifting in the current. A shadow emerges ahead — vast, murky, and accelerating — until it becomes a monstrous, razor-filled maw, punctuated by cold black eyes.

When she was young, she'd wake up screaming. But over time, fear gave way to fascination. Somehow, she knew these creatures were real. Or had been. Or could be.

She searched every Subnet Data Store for anything resembling the predators in her dreams. Nothing came close. So she set out to create them herself.

Jean's rise as an Evolutionary Simulationist was meteoric. She squealed with delight when she pioneered techniques for using sentient viruses to approximate biological adaptation. Now, a deeper question consumes her:

If soul code froze humanity's essence, what potential was lost? What might they have become, given time, given pressure, given the freedom to evolve? In her dreams, she's learned to adapt. To survive. But that isn't enough. Jean wants to become something more. And for that, she'll need powerful tools, ancient, dangerous, and buried deep in the Old Code.
