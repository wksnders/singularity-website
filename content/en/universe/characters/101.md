epithet: 'Cyber Deity'

Long ago, there was no moon in the digital sky. Before the nature of simulated reality was understood, various gods and goddesses were prominently present in the minds of Autumn Network's inhabitants. One night, a queen — wise and radiant — offered prayers to a silent lupine god, seeking inspiration to move her subjects to greatness. That evening, she had a vision of shining radiance contrasted sharply against the night sky.

She wrapped herself in her finest robes, woven from silver threadlight and pastel silks, and climbed to the highest spire atop the tallest tower of her castle. There, in the early morning light, a few of her subjects spotted her silhouette against the muted sky — proud, elegant, and still. They smiled, and she smiled back.

Emboldened, she climbed once more to her perch the following night. Word spread quickly of the silver queen who watched over her people. Her subjects would look up to the top of the tower as they went about their nightly errands, quietly beaming and empowered beneath her encouraging gaze. As she saw her steadfast vigil inspire her people, she began to stay atop the roof longer and longer, through both long days and long nights.

Eventually, she never came down again. And now, the server has a moon. Some say it watches still.
