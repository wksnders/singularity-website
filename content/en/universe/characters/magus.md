---
name: 'Magus'
epithet: 'Wish Bringer'
---

Magus remembers the soaring white spires of pristine, uncarved digital reality — the truly ancient times, when different kinds of magic were alive. He remembers the ambition, and the hubris of those who lived in those spires, including his own. He remembers the wild power they wielded, and the digital landscape lurching and groaning as it reluctantly bent to their whims. Magus is old. Old code.

Everything existing now is new by comparison, endless flitting sparks blinking in and out over a sea of server time. The old world is forgotten, but not lost. It lies dormant, poised to resurface, hidden beneath the rocks, beyond the horizon, behind the stars. One shard of it pokes through the divide, quietly calling to be found.

The Singularity fragment hunters are close, brilliant young minds, blindly probing at the edge of outcomes they cannot fathom. Magus holds his silence, for the only concept more dangerous than the Singularity itself is a real understanding of what it can do. Once again, he's called to action, to seal the breach, and to rebury a dead past. Old Code must only be met with old code, and even that is too much. He only hopes his ancient bearing can keep pace.
