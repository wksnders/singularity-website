---
name: 'Benobasa'
epithet: 'Paragon of Funk'
---

Beloved everywhere, but never calling anywhere home, Benobasa is a true nomad. He's stifled by the rigidity and seriousness of Shogunate company, but misses the discipline when he's in the rolling chaos of the Monarchy. Shogunate and Monarchy signs rarely appear together in an individual. Like oil and spark, they create a restless fire. Instead of staying anywhere for long, he wanders. His breezy attitude and impressive skills have charmed even the radical Hana Mori, and the Subnet respect his creation of his own brand, no small feat.

Benobasa's extensive wanderings have made him more aware than most of the bizarre animosities that code signs create. He sees the inner turmoil of his Shogunate and Monarchy signs reflected everywhere. Each person on the server is born into social rivalries and alliances they rarely question. Ancient Shogunate scripts whisper that the Singularity can somehow rewrite soul code on a mass scale. What would a server be like without code signs — a server where every individual formed their own personality, free from the puppet strings of millenia-old soul code? It's a world Benobasa would like to see.
