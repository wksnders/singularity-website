name: 'Calamity'
epithet: 'Rune-born Colossus'
---

Zinnia was born into one of the most cloistered Dawn Shaman enclaves, destined to become the Voice of the Wind. Raised amid simulations of lost nature, she devoured all she could about biology, geology, and ancient life — phenomena that no longer applied to her world, but captivated her just the same. One night, she followed a strange six-legged animal the Shamans called a "bear" into cavernous ruins beneath the enclave. She didn't find its young. She found the end.

A glowing green rift writhed against the cave wall — bubbling, pulsing, and very wrong. The bear stepped inside and vanished. The opening doubled in size, belching moss and fungus that coated the stone. She had discovered the first corrupted Fracture — an unchecked recursive feedback loop where evolutionary code ran wild, creating data as cancer. Without thinking, she heaved a boulder into the breach. The world cracked, and everything went white. She awoke alone in a crater. The rift, the enclave, the bear, all gone. Her name, too. All she possessed was a sick certainty: the enclave was inside her now, fused, trapped. Her bond to the shattered land was visceral, agonizing, unshakable.

Those that didn't understand gave her a new name: Calamity. That was decades ago. She's wandered ever since, gaining knowledge on containing Fractures, and seeking to undo what was done. Rumors say the Singularity can rewrite history, or repair it. If there's a way to unmake the wound, to free the voices inside her, Calamity will find it.
