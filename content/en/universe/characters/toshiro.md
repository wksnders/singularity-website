name: 'Toshiro'
epithet: 'Unmatched Ronin'
---

Toshiro only refers to himself as the Betrayer. He claims no lineage, but the oldest on the server know his stance belies the Shogunate's Sixth House. Others only know his undying devotion to defending Shogunate territory from the darkest existential threats.

The memories of nearly four centuries before still burn brightly in Toshiro's mind. He remembers infiltrating the Imperial Reliquary late at night, and taking the hungering blade, Muramasa, from its pedestal. He remembers dividing his master, Black Shizo, cleanly down the middle after he ordered the massacre at Ryuki Bridge. He remembers the two halves burning away in a maelstrom of shimmering fire, crashing forever. He remembers the Shizo's still-cindering data drifting on the wind toward the Broken Heap.

Toshiro was willing to pay any price to the Muramasa to end Shizo's reign of terror — limb, sanity, or soul. The Muramasa found another cost. It stole someone from him. While the Muramasa divided great evil from the world that day, the blade divided good in equal measure. Toshiro learned that there was in fact, one cost too great.

Toshiro the betrayer. Honor, station, tradition, lineage, loyalty, love — betrayed. The Old Code of the Muramasa is irreversible and absolute. At least, that's what most assume. There may be a program older still, more powerful, that can revert the Faustian bargain he made. One day, he'll see if the name Toshiro fits again.
