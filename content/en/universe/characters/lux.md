---
name: 'LuX'
epithet: 'Druidic Seeker'
---

A legendary relic-hunter from the Hana Mori, LuX scours the server for lost code, experimental builds, and forbidden functions, not to preserve them, but to use them. She's a daring archaeologist, and she's willing to work with any faction if it means securing a prized prototype.

She's not chasing the Singularity. She's chasing legacy.

While the whole server scrambles to assemble the Singularity, LuX is on a very different kind of hunt. Ancient Shogunate logs describe the strange, incomprehensible properties of the Endian Keys, but just like hundreds of other programmatic artifacts, no one knows what their true purpose is.

LuX was present at their creation. The Endian Keys open a vault — a vault that's inside her. Part of her is locked within, and she wants it back.
