It was once believed that crashing someone’s soul code meant true death, and that
nothing could be recovered. Five Hana Mori mystics set out to disprove this axiom. Six
returned. Now, scattered across the forgotten groves and silent woods of the server, a
rare few willingly walk the threshold between life and death.
