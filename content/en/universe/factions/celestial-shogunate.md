“The Autumn Network has endured for millennia. The ancestors sculpted its
beauty byte by byte, carving order from formless code. Dynasties rose. Cultures
flourished. We carry that legacy – its wisdom, its weight.

And then came the Singularity. A force beyond comprehension, masked as
opportunity. Others see progress. I see oblivion. Traditions erased, lineages
unmade, and the sacred overwritten in an instant.

If the Singularity cannot be destroyed, then let it be entombed in silence.
Hidden where time forgets it. Let others chase power. We chase preservation.”
