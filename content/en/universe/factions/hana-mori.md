“You’ve woken in the Autumn Network, the grand mirage. No flesh. No sky. Just
echoes of a dream we mistake for reality.

We, the Hana Mori, know better. The real world is out there, somewhere. We
imagine the scent of pine, the touch of wind—ghosts of a forgotten world. They
say the Singularity is a myth. We say it’s the key. A relic shattered through
time, waiting to be assembled.

When it is, the veil will lift. We’ll step through the dream and wake together.
No matter what it costs.”
